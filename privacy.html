Privacy Policy

This policy describes the intended TikTok draft-upload workflow of HarborMediaStudio for AhaHarbor. The integration is being configured for internal use; it is not a public SaaS service.

Purpose and data

TikTok authorization is used solely to connect the authorized AhaHarbor account to the internal publishing workflow. The workflow processes only the account information and authorization credentials needed to authenticate that connection, and the selected video files, upload identifiers and status information needed to carry out authorized draft uploads.

Limited use and disclosure

We do not sell TikTok data or use it for advertising, profiling, or tracking. TikTok integration data is not shared with unrelated third parties. Selected content and the information necessary to authenticate and perform the upload are communicated to TikTok, which processes them under its own policies.

Credentials and account control

OAuth tokens and client secrets belong in the private internal workflow, never in this public website, its repository, or public logs. This website does not collect TikTok credentials. The account owner controls the authorized connection and can revoke the application's access through TikTok's account settings. Revoking access does not by itself delete content already transferred to TikTok; that content is managed in TikTok.

This website

These static pages contain no tracking code, analytics, advertising, forms, or site-set cookies. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub receives ordinary web-request information, such as an IP address, to serve the pages. This hosting is separate from the internal TikTok workflow; no TikTok tokens or video files are published here.

See GitHub's Privacy Statement and TikTok's Privacy Policy for those providers' practices.

Internal data management

The account owner manages internal workflow records and credentials in the private production workspace. This informational website does not provide account-registration, automated deletion, or support-ticket services.
