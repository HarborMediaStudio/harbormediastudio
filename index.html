A draft-first publishing workflow

We are setting up an internal publishing integration to transfer our own finished English-language AhaHarbor videos to TikTok using the video.upload permission. Uploads are intended for the draft workflow, where the account owner reviews the video and completes publication manually in TikTok.

A person makes the final publishing decision.
The intended integration uploads videos for review. It does not automatically publish them.

Internal use only

HarborMediaStudio is not a public software-as-a-service platform. This website provides information about the studio and its internal workflow; it offers no sign-up, TikTok login, or video-upload form.
