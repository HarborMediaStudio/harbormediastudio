Terms of Service

Scope

HarborMediaStudio is an internal content-production and publishing system for HarborMediaStudio and AhaHarbor. It is not a public SaaS offering. Use of the publishing workflow is restricted to authorized studio and account-owner activities.

Authorized content and accounts

Only connect accounts you are authorized to manage. Only upload content owned by HarborMediaStudio or content for which the necessary permissions and publication rights have been obtained. Content must comply with TikTok's applicable rules.

Drafts and manual publication

The intended TikTok integration transfers finished videos through the draft-upload workflow using video.upload. A successful upload is not a public post. The account owner must manually review the draft and decide whether to publish it in TikTok. This integration does not authorize automatic public posting.

TikTok is independent

TikTok is an independent third-party platform, not the operator of HarborMediaStudio. TikTok's own terms, policies, permissions and technical restrictions apply in addition to these internal-use terms. TikTok determines the availability of its services and the handling of content on its platform.

See TikTok's Terms of Service.

Privacy and credentials

Use the workflow consistently with our Privacy Policy. Authorization credentials must remain private and must not be added to the public website or its repository.
